Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Racked: Plate Calc & Gym Timer ("Racked", "the app", "we") is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Racked does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that phone home. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data we collect

None that leaves your device. Racked has no account system and no server. It never asks for your name, email, or location.

Data stored on your device

To make the app work, Racked saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone using the system's standard preferences storage (UserDefaults):

  • Your settings, such as bar weight, available plates, preferred unit (kg or lbs), and whether Live Activities are enabled.
  • A count of your training days and the app version, used only to decide when to show an optional App Store review prompt.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Required Reason API disclosure

Apple requires apps to declare certain system APIs. Racked uses UserDefaults (reason CA92.1) solely to read and write your settings and preferences on your own device. It is not used for tracking and is not linked to any identity.

Tracking

Racked does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

Purchases

Racked is free. It offers one optional in-app tip to support development. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children's privacy

Racked does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
