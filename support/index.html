Frequently asked questions

Can't reach the exact target weight?

If your available plates can't make the target exactly, Racked shows the actual Loaded Weight so you know the bar won't match. Adjust your available plates in Settings, or change the target.

How do I switch between kg and lbs?

Open Settings and change the unit. Bar weight, plate steps, and the default plate set all update to match.

The rest timer Live Activity isn't showing.

Make sure "Enable Live Activities" is on in Settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for Racked in iOS Settings → Racked. Only one rest timer runs at a time, so logging a new set updates the existing one rather than starting a second.

Does Racked collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your device, with no accounts, tracking, or analytics. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What's the tip for?

Racked is free. The optional in-app tip is a way to support ongoing development if you find the app useful — it doesn't unlock anything.
